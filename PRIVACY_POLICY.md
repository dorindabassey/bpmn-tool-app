# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 28-07-2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the **BPMN Miro Integration App** ("App") handles your information.

## 1. No Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or analyze any personal data.

- BPMN files are processed entirely within your Miro board session.
- No files or content are uploaded to external servers.
- We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking scripts.

## 2. File Handling

Any `.bpmn` or `.xml` files you upload are read locally within the browser for the sole purpose of rendering them in your Miro workspace. They are **not** stored or sent externally.

## 3. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties because **no data is collected** in the first place.

## 4. User Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring the content you import into Miro complies with your organization’s data handling and confidentiality policies.

## 5. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App changes. Updates will be posted here.
